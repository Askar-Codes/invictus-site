Memberships and Pricing

Invictus GO offers flexible membership options to fit almost any schedule and budget. New visitors can try the club with a 3-visit trial pack for just 2,990 KZT — a great way to test out the equipment and the atmosphere before committing further. For those ready to make training a habit, our annual membership costs 219,000 KZT and includes unlimited access to the gym floor for a full year.

Every membership includes free locker usage and free access to all group training sessions we run throughout the week, so you never have to carry your gym bag around or pay extra to join a class. As one of our trainers put it, health and fitness are the foundation of a happy and successful life — and we try to make that as easy as possible to act on.
